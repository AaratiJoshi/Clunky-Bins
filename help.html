How to register?


(1) For Registration, click on the registration button that is dispaly on upper side.

(2) So you can see one registration form on the screen,now choose your category.

(3) Now feelup the form according the questions.

(4) At last click on the "submit" button and your registration is completed.

How to Login?


(1) For Login in website, click on the login button that is dispaly on upper side.

(2) Then choose your category(Admin or User).

(3) So you can see one login form on the screen,now again choose your category and write your username and password that you can give in your registration form and click on the "submit" button.

(4) If you forget the password then click on the "Reset password option" & reset the password.
